Crypto Arbitrage Scanner — local, browser-only, no install

Runs entirely in this browser tab. Every request below goes directly from your machine to the exchange, not through any server of mine — I never see your traffic. Close the tab to stop.
What this measures, and what it does NOT prove: this scanner compares live top-of-book (best bid/ask) prices across exchanges and computes the net edge after each exchange's taker fee. That's it. It does NOT account for: slippage below the top of the book on larger size, network/execution latency between seeing a price and actually trading it, the time and price risk of moving funds between exchanges for cross-exchange arbitrage, or competition from faster bots already working the same spread. A detected "opportunity" here is evidence something is worth investigating further — it is not evidence you could have captured it. Run this for a while (15–30+ minutes), export the log, and send it back for an honest read on how often/how large/how persistent anything real-looking actually was.
